About Plainpick

Plainpick is an independent publisher of product comparisons and travel guides. Our pages are researched with software assistance and reviewed by a human editor before publication. Every hard fact is cited to its source and dated; where we could not verify something, we say so instead of guessing.

Some outbound links may become partner links that pay us a commission at no cost to you. Commissions never change a verdict: our comparisons include products that pay us nothing, and every product page lists honest downsides.

Corrections: if you spot an error, email plainpick@ the domain of this site's repository owner.
