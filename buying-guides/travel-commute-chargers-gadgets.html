1. The Single Brick Solution: Anker Prime 67W GaN Charger

Carrying separate bulky power bricks for laptops, smartphones, and smartwatches is unnecessary with modern Gallium Nitride (GaN) semiconductors. The Anker Prime 67W provides two USB-C ports and one USB-A port in a form factor 51% smaller than original laptop chargers.

Highlights

  • Dynamic PowerIQ 4.0: Intelligently allocates power (e.g. 45W to your laptop and 20W to your phone simultaneously).
  • ActiveShield 2.0: Continuous thermal monitoring prevents dangerous heat buildup during extended fast charging sessions.
  • Saudi Standard: Available with official 3-pin Type G UK plugs that plug securely into standard Saudi wall sockets.

2. High-Power Laptop Battery: Anker 737 Power Bank (PowerCore 24K)

When working from airport lounges, cafes, or remote locations, the Anker 737 delivers full 140W bi-directional Power Delivery 3.1.

Highlights

  • Real-Time Smart Display: Shows live input/output wattage, estimated recharge time, and battery health percentage.
  • Massive Capacity: 24,000mAh capacity provides over 4.5 full charges for an iPhone 15 Pro or 1.3 full charges for a MacBook Air.
  • Aviation Compliant: Rated at 86.4Wh, fully compliant with TSA and IATA airline carry-on regulations.

3. Road Emergency Essentials: Baseus 1000A Jump Starter & Xiaomi 1S Inflator

Extreme GCC summer heat accelerates vehicle battery degradation and affects tire pressure. Keeping emergency equipment in your trunk provides total self-reliance.

  • Baseus 1000A Jump Starter: Starts gasoline engines up to 6.0L with spark-proof intelligent clamps. Holds standby charge for up to 9 months and doubles as an emergency power bank and bright flashlight.
  • Xiaomi 1S Portable Compressor: Cordless lithium-powered pump with precision digital pressure gauge (±1 psi accuracy). Automatically shuts off when the pre-set tire pressure (e.g. 35 psi) is reached.
